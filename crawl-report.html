5. What this does not tell

  • These are live pages, fetched from the sitemaps. A page behind a login, or a page that failed to download, is not here (see the crawl status in section 1).
  • Only the main content of a page was scanned (the entry content or the Elementor page). Header, footer and the head are not included; a page-level style in the head is not counted.
  • Counts show how much of the site a component touches, not how many different designs exist. A widget in a looped template is counted every time.
  • The merges of widget types into components, and the split of custom blocks into static, light and application, are judged from the markup. A person must confirm them.
  • "Can convert" says a widget can follow the normal route (screenshots, widget map, component). It does not say the component exists, or that it will match every look. Each component still has to pass the screenshot and behaviour checks.
  • Products that are not built with Elementor widgets (or pages without them) show few widgets; that is a finding about the builder, not an error.
